Home rota

You left. Your homeboy stayed with the swannery and the grumphies. The app is the feeding rota you still share, not a postcard of the birds.

Open today’s pen

What you actually do

Mark the swan pen and the pig pen for the day. If the sow is out, or a swan is off the water, the friend who is there already knows. You are confirming the rota, not watching a stream.

What it will not do

It will not diagnose an animal, sell a feed, or show you a phone with two shop buttons. Grumphie here is the Scots word for the pig, used because that is what you called her.
