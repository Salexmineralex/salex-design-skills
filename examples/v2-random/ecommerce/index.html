Method

A lot opens when the producer wires the affineur’s note, the GPS of the workshop, and the weight. We print that as a label. If we cannot name the person and the neck, we do not list the lot. That is the whole anti-fake.

We do not sell copies of famous inland names. Grouin is Grouin. Tarbert is Tarbert.
